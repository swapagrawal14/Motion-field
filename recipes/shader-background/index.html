Recipe 11 · Micro-interactions & backgrounds

A background that breathes.

A full-bleed WebGL fragment shader — layered noise flowing into a cyan glow, bending toward the pointer. No library, one context, paused the moment it leaves the viewport.

weight · small deps · WebGL (no library) trigger · time + pointer pauses offscreen

WebGL · one fragment shader · no library

Light you can
scroll through.

Layered value noise flows through a deep navy field, curls into cyan and warms around the pointer. It is painted on one canvas, at device pixel ratio capped at 1.5, and it stops when you scroll past it.

running · 60 fps
Use it for

One ambient moment

A hero, a CTA backdrop, a section divider. It sets a mood without competing with type. Keep it behind content with a scrim, and never behind body copy at low contrast.

Knobs

Tune the field

  • u_t * 0.12 flow speed
  • fbm(..., 5) octaves — 4 is cheaper
  • pow(f, 3.0) * 0.5 glow amount
  • 0.10 * exp(-d * 7.0) pointer halo
  • 1.5 max device pixel ratio
Reduced motion & perf

One still frame

Reduced motion hides the canvas and shows the CSS gradient (or renders a single frame if WebGL is on). The loop pauses offscreen and on visibilitychange; DPR is capped and the canvas is low-power.
