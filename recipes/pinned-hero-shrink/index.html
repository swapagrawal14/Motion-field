Recipe 12 · Big moments

Full bleed, then a card.

The hero holds the viewport while the page scrolls underneath, then folds into a rounded frame as the headline lifts away. One scroll-linked beat that gives the whole page its opening note.

weight · small deps · none trigger · scroll-linked (pinned) one per page

Pinned hero

The page keeps moving
after you stop.

Hold the frame. Let the copy leave first. What remains is a card the rest of the page can sit beside.

Now the page continues, unhurried.

Scroll back up and the hero unfolds again.

Use it for

The opening frame

Product launches, portfolios, campaign pages — anywhere the first screen should feel like a title card that the page then leaves behind. One per site, at the very top.

Knobs

Tune the fold

  • scale(1 - p*0.16) final size
  • borderRadius p*30px frame rounding
  • opacity 1 - p*1.9 copy leaves early
  • height: 220vh scroll length (170 on mobile)
Reduced motion & perf

A normal hero

With reduced motion the section is height: auto, the media is full-bleed and the copy stays put — no pinning, no transform. Only transform, opacity and border-radius change per frame.
