How it reads

The page moves down. The story moves sideways. Two rows travel in opposite directions at different rates, so the band gains depth without a single 3D transform.

Use it for

Stories with a direction

Process bands, chapter breaks, manifesto lines, logo strips between sections. One or two bands per page is plenty.

Knobs

Tune the travel

  • data-drift distance in vw (sign = direction)
  • EASE 0.08 floaty → 1 locked
  • Opposite signs on stacked rows add depth
Reduced motion & perf

Still, and cheap

With reduced motion every row sits at 0. Only transform changes; rows outside the viewport are skipped and the rAF loop stops once settled.
