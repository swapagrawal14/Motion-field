Recipe 13 · Big moments

Scroll down, travel sideways.

Vertical scroll drives a sideways gallery: the section pins, the track travels, and a progress bar keeps you oriented. On phones and reduced motion it becomes a native swipe row with snap points.

weight · small deps · none trigger · scroll-linked (pinned) native swipe on mobile

Use it for

Galleries with a sequence

Work showcases, product tours, step-by-step narratives, portfolios. Five panels is plenty; past that people lose track of where they are.

Knobs

Tune the travel

  • 100vh + extra section height from the track width
  • 40vw panel width
  • 0.14 lerp — lower floats more
  • scroll-snap-type: x mandatory on mobile
Reduced motion & perf

Swipe, don't hijack

On mobile and reduced motion the section is a native horizontal scroll row with snap points — no pinned height, no transform. Desktop writes only transform on the track and one height value on the section.
