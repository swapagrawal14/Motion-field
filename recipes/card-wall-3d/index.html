Recipe 14 · Big moments

A wall of work, seen in perspective.

A perspective-tilted wall of preview cards: columns drift in opposite directions as you scroll and the whole plane leans a few degrees toward the pointer. Reads as depth, costs one transform.

Use it for

Showreels and work walls

A portfolio's project index, a studio's capabilities wall, a product's feature mosaic. It says "there is a lot here" without a grid that looks like a spreadsheet.

Knobs

Tune the wall

  • rotateX(13deg) rotateY(-16deg) rotateZ(3deg) base plane
  • DIR = [1, -1, 1] column drift directions
  • 26px drift distance
  • 0.08 pointer lerp
Reduced motion & perf

A flat wall

Below 700 px and under reduced motion the plane is flat and columns do not drift. Desktop writes only transform on one plane and three columns; the loop stops when settled.
