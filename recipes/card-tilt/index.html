Recipe 09 · Micro-interactions & backgrounds

Depth you can feel under the pointer.

Cards tilt in 3D toward the pointer with a moving glare, and settle flat when you leave. Keyboard focus applies the same tilt, so the effect is never pointer-only.

Use it for

Work and product cards

Case-study grids, product shots, team cards — anything with an image that benefits from a little parallax. Keep the tilt under ~10°; past that it reads as a gimmick.

Knobs

Tune the tilt

  • MAX 9 degrees at the edges
  • translateZ(30px) lifts the art off the card
  • --gx / --gy glare position
  • 0.18 lerp — lower floats more
Reduced motion & perf

Flat, but still lit

Reduced motion removes the tilt and the glare, keeps the border and the focus ring. The card stays overflow: hidden so nothing spills into the page, and only transform animates.
