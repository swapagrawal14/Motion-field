Recipe 06 · Scroll-linked & entrances

Cards that breathe when you arrive.

On hover or keyboard focus the card lifts, its border catches the accent and a ring pulses out once — then keeps a soft breathing glow until you leave. Motion as feedback, not decoration.

weight · tiny deps · none trigger · hover + focus keyboard parity
01 · Hover

Instant landing

The card answers the pointer in under 120 ms, so the page feels alive before anyone clicks.

02 · Glow

One accent, used sparingly

A radial highlight follows the pointer. It is the same cyan as every other move on the page.

03 · Ring

A single pulse

The ring expands from the centre and fades. It marks the moment of arrival; it does not loop forever.

04 · Keyboard

Focus is a hover

Tab to a card and the same glow, lift and ring appear. Nothing is pointer-only.

05 · Edge

Border, not shadow

A 1 px accent edge reads cleaner than a heavy drop shadow on a dark surface.

06 · Reduced

Still legible

With reduced motion the lift, glow and ring are off — the card keeps its border and focus ring.

Use it for

Grids that respond

Feature cards, pricing tiers, team cards, link lists — anywhere a grid of similar boxes needs to tell you which one you are on. Keep it to cards that are actually interactive or informative.

Knobs

Tune the pulse

  • scale(16) ring size — lower for a tighter beat
  • 1.15s ring period; one ring per arrival
  • --mx / --my pointer-follow highlight
  • translateY(-4px) lift
Reduced motion & perf

Feedback without movement

Reduced motion removes the lift, glow and ring but keeps the accent border and a visible focus ring. The ring animates only while hovered, and pointer handlers are passive.
