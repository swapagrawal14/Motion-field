Recipe 21 · Sound

A page you can hear — if you ask.

Procedural WebAudio pops, ticks and whooshes on hover and click. No audio files, no autoplay: sound stays muted until the visitor turns it on, and every sound is synthesized the moment it plays.

weight · tiny deps · none trigger · hover + click muted by default
Muted by default · turn it on, then hover and click
muted

One file: assets/ui-sound.js. Hover a card for the light sound, click it for the main one. The AudioContext is created on the first opt-in, never on load.

Use it for

Feedback you can hear

Hover ticks on nav and cards, a pop on the primary CTA, a whoosh on a drawer or page wipe, a two-note success on a form. Keep volumes low (0.05–0.2) and never layer more than one sound at a time.

Knobs

Tune the kit

  • data-sfx="pop|tick|whoosh|plip|success|error" on click
  • data-sfx-hover="tick" for the light sound
  • UIsound.setMuted(false) to enable programmatically
  • gain peaks in FX (0.03–0.2)
Reduced motion & perf

Silent until asked

Muted by default; the AudioContext is created on the first toggle, so there is no autoplay and no cost on load. Sounds are synthesized per event — zero bytes shipped — and a failed sound never breaks the page.
