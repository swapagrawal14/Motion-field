Word level · for section titles

One move, one meaning. Letters first, words second, paragraphs never.

Use it for

Titles that earn attention

Hero headlines, section openers, chapter numbers. Keep the sentence under about eight words — a long line staggered letter by letter takes too long.

Knobs

Tune the arrival

  • --i per character → 26ms stagger
  • translateY(.42em) lift; raise for more drama
  • blur(8px) → 0 softens the entrance
  • Word level: 70ms per word
Reduced motion & perf

Still and cheap

With reduced motion every character is visible with no animation. Characters are inline-block spans (no layout shift), and the entrance runs once — hover replays only the word under the pointer.
