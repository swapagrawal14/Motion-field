About

Design first, motion second, performance always.

I am a designer-developer working with studios and founders who want a site that feels considered. Every project starts with the content and the one action the page must earn; motion is the last pass, never the first idea.

Vanilla HTML, CSS and JS by default. Frameworks when they pay for themselves. Nothing shipped without a reduced-motion path and a clean QA report.

Process

Four steps, one line.

01

Listen

One page of questions. The action, the audience, the references, the thing we must not break.

02

Design

Tokens first, then type, then layout. Motion is named in the design doc, not improvised later.

03

Build

Static at both widths, then the motion pass, then the QA gates. Screenshots before opinions.

04

Tune

Trim what is loud. Keep one big moment per screen and let the rest stay quiet.
