Questions

Before you ask.

Is there parking?

Yes, a paid lot behind the venue and street parking after 18:00. The courtyard gate is a two-minute walk.

Can I get a refund?

Full refund until seven days before the event. After that, tickets are transferable but not refundable.

Is it accessible?

Step-free entry, accessible toilets and a reserved seating row. Write to us and we will hold a space.
